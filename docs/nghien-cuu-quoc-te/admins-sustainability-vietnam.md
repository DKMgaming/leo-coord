# Note: Space sustainability (index, read 2026-10-19)
- IADC Space Debris Mitigation Guidelines (2002; Rev 1 2007; Rev 2 2020; Rev 3 2021): LEO objects: deorbit (direct reentry preferred) or manoeuvre to orbit with residual lifetime <=25 years; disposal success probability >=90%; for large constellations shorter lifetime/higher probability may be needed (guideline 5.3.2). Passivation (5.2), break-up avoidance (5.1), collision avoidance: estimate/limit accidental collision probability; avoidance manoeuvres if reliable data available (5.4). GEO graveyard: perigee raise >= 235 km x (1000 x CR x A/m), e <= 0.003. Source: https://sup1qywlxpbg9wylvrlmvhrlopvprl3qi.vcatalyst.top/library/iadc-space-debris-guidelines-revision-2.pdf and https://www.talksatellite.com/EMEA2331.html
- Trend: FCC 5-year rule (Sept 2022) tightens LEO disposal; EU Space Act proposal (2025): 90% disposal success target, mandatory collision-avoidance services; COPUOS Long-term Sustainability Guidelines (2019, 21 guidelines) - background.

# Note: Vietnam (index, read 2026-10-19)
- Law on Radio Frequencies (Law No. 42/2009/QH12, amended 2018): licence types include "frequency and satellite orbit use licences" (giay phep su dung tan so va quy dao ve tinh); Authority of Radio Frequency Management (Cuc Tan so vo tuyen dien, ARFM) under Ministry of Science and Technology (formerly MIC). Source: https://webiis08.mondaq.com/social-media/1288674/tmt-comparative-guide
- Decision 137/2006/QD-TTg (June 14, 2006): established Vietnam Committee for Aerospace Technology Research and Application (Vietnam Space Committee), assisting PM; committee set up 2010, enhanced Jan 2013 with Defense Ministry reps. Source: https://english.luatvietnam.vn/decision-no-137-2006-qd-ttg-dated-june-14-2006-of-the-prime-minister-approving-the-strategy-on-research-into-and-application-of-aerospace-technolo-18791-doc1.html
- Space science-tech strategy to 2030: signed by PM Nguyen Xuan Phuc (Decision 169/QD-TTg, Feb 4, 2021): targets 300 experts + 3,000 engineers by 2030, ~10 specialized labs, master small-sat assembly/integration. Source: https://www.roc-taiwan.org/vn_en/post/3984.html
- Decision 659/QD-TTg (Mar 26, 2025): 5-year pilot of LEO satellite telecom services without foreign ownership limits, concluding before Jan 1, 2031; Starlink licensed: 4 gateway stations + up to 600,000 user terminals.
