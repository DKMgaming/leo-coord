# Note: FCC NGSO FSS Report and Order 2017
URL: https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-346893A1.pdf (FCC news release, read 2026-10-19, index)
- Document: Report and Order and FNPRM, FCC 17-122, IB Docket No. 16-408, adopted Sept 26, 2017.
- Amended US Table of Allocations to accommodate NGSO/GSO in Ka-band (20/30 GHz).
- Streamlined NGSO milestone rules: 50% deployment within 6 years, 100% within 9 years of license grant (replacing prior 100%-in-6-years rule); failure -> authorization reduced to number of satellites actually deployed.
- Eliminated international geographic coverage requirements.
- Adopted new default spectrum-sharing threshold mechanism absent coordination agreement between NGSO operators.
